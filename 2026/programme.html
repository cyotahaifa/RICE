    06 · PRESENTING AUTHORS

    Guidelines for presenting authors

    12 minper paper in total
    10 minpresentation
    2 minquestions and answers
    10 minjoin before the session starts

    Presentation

    • Use the official RICE 2026 presentation template (PPTX). Ten to twelve slides in 16:9 suit ten minutes.
    • Slides may be PowerPoint or PDF. You will share your own screen, so keep the file open before the session begins.
    • All presentations must be in English.
    • The Session Chair will signal the eight-minute mark and close the talk at ten minutes. Please keep strictly to time.

    Joining the session

    • RICE 2026 is fully online. Rooms in this programme are online rooms, not physical ones.
    • Join ten minutes before the session start time, using the joining link given against your session.
    • Rename yourself as PaperID – Your Name (for example, 922541 – Rajiv Kumar). Chairs admit participants by this name.
    • Keep your camera on during your presentation, and stay for the whole session, not only your own slot.

    IEEE presentation requirement

    • As required by IEEE, a paper must be presented at the conference by one of its authors to be submitted to IEEE Xplore®. A paper that is not presented (a no-show) will not be submitted, and no refund or claim is admissible.
    • The presentation must be live and given by the registered author. Pre-recorded videos and presentation by a person who is not an author are not accepted.
    • Present only in your allotted session and time slot. Requests to change a session will be considered only if received by email by 3 November 2026.

    Technical arrangements

    • Test your audio, video and internet connection well in advance. A headset and a stable connection are recommended.
    • A failure of audio, video or internet at the author's end is treated as absence. Keep a co-author on standby where possible.
    • If you are disconnected, rejoin at once. The chair will accommodate you at the end of the session if time permits.
    • Any problem must be raised by email to riceconvenor@gmail.com during the allotted session, with a screenshot showing the time.

    Certificates

    • E-certificates are emailed after the presentation, in the name of the registered author who presented.
    • Additional certificates for co-authors are issued only if they were selected and paid for at registration.

    Conduct and recording

    • Participants are expected to follow the IEEE Code of Ethics and to treat every speaker with courtesy.
    • Sessions may be recorded for the conference record. Please do not record or circulate another author's presentation.
    07 · SESSION CHAIRS

    Guidelines for session chairs

    Each session has two chairs. Session Chair 1 conducts the session, keeps time and moderates the discussion. Session Chair 2 manages the online room: admitting participants, links and technical difficulty.

    Before and during the session

    • Join fifteen minutes early, and admit participants named in the format PaperID – Name.
    • Call papers in the order printed. Allow 10 minutes for the talk and 2 for questions, and signal the eight-minute mark.
    • Confirm that the presenter is an author of the paper. Do not accept recordings or proxy presenters.
    • An absent author's slot is not given to another paper. If time permits, call the absent paper again at the end.

    Evaluation sheet

    • Complete the session evaluation sheet: attendance (Present / Late / Absent), who presented, and scores out of 10 for technical content, presentation and Q&A.
    • Nominate at most one or two outstanding papers for the Best Paper Award.
    • Email the completed sheet to riceconvenor@gmail.com within one hour of the session ending, with the subject “Session Sxx evaluation”.
